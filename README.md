## What is PodText.ai?

**[PodText](https://podtext.ai)** is an AI-powered tool that turns any **podcast, audio, or video into a clean, searchable, exportable text transcript** — in seconds, with no downloads or uploads required.

Paste a link from **Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Xiaoyuzhou (小宇宙), YouTube**, or any public RSS/audio URL, and PodText fetches the episode, transcribes it with state-of-the-art speech-to-text AI, and gives you a transcript you can **search, summarize, and export** — as SRT/VTT subtitles, plain text, or Markdown show notes.

You can also upload your own audio or video files (interviews, meetings, lectures, recordings) directly.

**[Try it free →](https://podtext.ai)** No credit card required. 60 minutes free.

Yes — every new account gets 60 minutes of free transcription, no credit card required.

**What platforms does PodText support?**
